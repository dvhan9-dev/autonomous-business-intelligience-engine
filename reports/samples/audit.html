Assumptions and limitations

  • Sprint 1 demonstration: synthetic normalized USD inputs only; not a native Amazon export adapter.
  • All costs, discounts, refunds and attributed sales are period totals; selling price is per unit. COGS excludes separately supplied shipping and inbound freight. Fulfillment fees exclude those inputs.
  • Net sales = units × selling price − discounts − refunds. Gross profit = net sales − COGS − shipping − inbound freight.
  • Contribution profit = gross profit − referral fees − fulfillment fees − storage fees − advertising spend. Contribution margin = contribution profit / net sales. This is not accounting net income.
  • ACOS = advertising spend / advertising-attributed sales; TACOS = advertising spend / net sales. Attributed sales overlap total sales and are NEVER added to revenue. Match attribution windows before use.
  • Coverage estimate = inventory / supplied historical daily sales velocity. Reorder estimate = ceil(velocity × lead time); at risk when inventory ≤ reorder estimate and velocity > 0. Estimates assume stable demand and lead time, no inbound inventory or safety stock; medium confidence when inputs exist. Zero velocity means undefined coverage and no observed demand, not infinite coverage.
  • Missing inputs stay null and propagate to dependent calculations; actual zeros stay zero. Ratios with nonpositive denominators are undefined (null). Money is rounded half up to cents; ratios/coverage to four decimals. Totals sum displayed SKU values.
  • Heuristic review thresholds: margin <10%, TACOS >20%, coverage >120 days. They are investigation triggers, not universal targets. No competitor financial metrics are inferred.
  • Storage fees are observed inputs; inventory carrying cost, capital cost, price elasticity, incremental ad returns and profit uplift are not estimated without additional evidence.
  • Local deterministic execution has $0.00 estimated external API cost; compute and human labor are excluded.
