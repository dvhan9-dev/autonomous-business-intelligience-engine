# Roadmap

## Sprint 1 — implemented foundation

A synthetic 24-SKU normalized import, validated deterministic financials, opportunities with evidence,
HTML/JSON output, local FastAPI/CLI, persisted task metadata, evaluator and proposal-only improvement loop.

## Sprint 2 — secure pilot and first paying customer validation

1. Founder approves a seller interview and an audit offer experiment. Record the $79 price as a hypothesis,
   success criteria, evidence and continue/pivot/terminate decision; do not auto-send outreach.
2. Build authentication and enforce tenant ownership, retention, secure storage and deletion before any
   real dataset is accepted. Complete the security prerequisites in `security.md`.
3. Obtain consented sample export schemas, build native import mappings, reconcile totals against seller
   statements and document attribution/refund windows. Preserve unknowns and original evidence references.
4. Add a generic experiment record (hypothesis, budget, approvals, metrics, evidence, decision) to the core.
5. Improve cost allocation, inventory age/inbound orders and backtesting; validate recommendations with
   human financial review. Quantify profit scenarios only with explicit assumptions and confidence.

## Later, evidence-dependent

Public competitor snapshots with attributed public sources and dated observations (no invented financials),
opt-in ongoing monitoring, billing and metering, multiple provider adapters/BYOK, persistent business memory,
local/cloud agents, durable queues, opportunity scoring, business templates and sandboxed workflow improvement.
Subscription pricing of $49–$99 is provisional. Product expansion depends on validated customer outcomes.
